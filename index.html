About Us

Circle of Support Inc. provides coordinated services and compassionate support to individuals and families navigating life’s challenges. We connect families with the resources they need today while planning together for a stronger tomorrow.

Our Services

Future Planning

Helping families set goals and plan for a brighter future.

Care Provider Coordination

Connecting families with trusted providers and community resources.

Emergency Planning

Preparing families for unexpected events with safety and peace of mind.

Regional Center Services

Supporting access to regional center programs and services.

Community-Based Support

Providing in-home and community supports that build independence.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and families through coordinated care, planning, and community partnerships—creating a circle where everyone belongs.

  • Experienced and compassionate staff

  • Commitment to empowerment and independence
