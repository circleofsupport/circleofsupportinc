## 📖 About This Project

This is the official website for **Circle of Support Inc.**, a nonprofit organization providing coordinated family support services to individuals and families navigating life's challenges.

Our mission is to empower individuals and families through coordinated care, planning, and community partnerships—creating a circle where everyone belongs.

---

## 🛠️ Services We Offer

- **Future Planning** – Helping families set goals and plan for a brighter future.
- **Regional Center Services** – Supporting access to regional center programs and services.
- **Care Provider Coordination** – Connecting families with trusted providers and community resources.
- **Emergency Planning** – Preparing families for unexpected events with safety and peace of mind.
- **Community-Based Support** – Providing in-home and community supports that build independence.
